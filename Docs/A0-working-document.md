## Internal project name
Orbital Rescue / 轨道救援

## Synopsis
A first-person Unity VR experience for students and general users interested in space exploration. The player is an astronaut aboard a station damaged by orbital debris. After a mission briefing, the player enters an exterior maintenance area, collects floating debris and reconnects a damaged power interface, then returns to conclude the mission. The intended experience takes five to ten minutes.

## Spaces and intended flow
1. Interior Mission Area: mission terminal, tools and space to prepare.
2. Airlock: hatch and transition to the exterior.
3. Exterior Maintenance Area: solar arrays, handrails, debris and power repair interface, against Earth and space.

## Intended interactions after A1
Grab, move and release objects using VR controllers. Travel along handrails outside the station. Collect debris and repair the power connection. Floating objects, environmental sound and equipment feedback provide visual and interactive cues of weightlessness. A headset cannot remove the user's physical sense of gravity; the concept explores perceived weightlessness through visual motion and interaction.

## A1 implementation boundary
A1 supplies a desktop walkthrough through an editable primitive blockout. It establishes dimensions and route feasibility. Hatches are open, debris and equipment are static placeholders, and the exterior deck is a walking inspection aid. VR locomotion, grabbing, repair logic, sound, final art and lighting are later work.
